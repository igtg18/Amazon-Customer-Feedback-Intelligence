# Business Insights

## Objective

The objective of the Business Insights stage is to convert the SQL analysis results into meaningful business observations.

The analysis focuses on customer satisfaction, category performance, negative-review patterns, product issues, and customer pain points.

## 1. Overall Customer Feedback

The integrated database contains 661,373 customer reviews across 5 product categories.

The overall average rating is approximately 4.01.

This provides a high-level view of customer feedback across the analyzed categories.

## 2. Category Performance

Average ratings were analyzed for each category:

| Category | Average Rating |
|---|---:|
| Books | 4.34 |
| Grocery | 4.17 |
| Ebook | 4.11 |
| Beauty | 3.96 |
| Electronics | 3.59 |

The category-level comparison helps identify differences in customer satisfaction across product categories.

## 3. Sentiment Analysis

Customer reviews were classified into positive, neutral, and negative sentiment.

Category-wise sentiment percentages were analyzed to understand how customer sentiment differs between product categories.

This helps identify categories that require deeper investigation into customer complaints and product issues.

## 4. Negative Review Analysis

Negative-review analysis was performed to identify categories with a higher proportion of negative customer feedback.

Electronics showed a comparatively high negative-review percentage in the SQL analysis.

The negative reviews were further investigated to identify the products and issues contributing to customer dissatisfaction.

## 5. Product-Level Issues

Products with higher numbers of negative reviews were identified using SQL aggregation.

This allows individual products generating repeated customer complaints to be investigated separately.

## 6. Customer Pain Points

Negative reviews were analyzed using issue-related keywords.

The analysis included:

- Quality
- Product condition
- Authenticity
- Price
- Delivery

These themes help identify the common areas mentioned by customers in negative feedback.

## 7. Actionable Business Interpretation

The findings can support business teams in:

- Identifying categories requiring deeper review
- Investigating products with repeated negative feedback
- Understanding common customer complaints
- Monitoring product quality concerns
- Investigating pricing-related complaints
- Reviewing delivery-related issues
- Improving customer experience

## 8. Next Step

The business insights identified from SQL analysis will be presented visually through the Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard will provide category comparisons, sentiment analysis, rating trends, and negative-review insights in an interactive format.
