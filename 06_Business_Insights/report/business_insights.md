# Business Insights Report

## 1. Category Rating Insights

The SQL analysis identified differences in average customer ratings across the five categories.

- Books: 4.34
- Grocery: 4.17
- Ebook: 4.11
- Beauty: 3.96
- Electronics: 3.59

The category comparison provides a starting point for identifying areas that may require further investigation.

## 2. Electronics Customer Feedback

Electronics recorded an average rating of 3.59 and a negative sentiment percentage of 29.52%.

The negative-review analysis identified recurring mentions related to product quality, product condition, price, and other product-related issues.

### Business interpretation

Electronics requires deeper investigation at the product level to understand which products and specific issues are contributing to negative customer feedback.

## 3. Beauty Customer Feedback

Beauty recorded an average rating of 3.96 with a negative sentiment percentage of 20.71%.

Negative reviews included mentions related to quality, product condition, authenticity, and price.

### Business interpretation

Beauty feedback can be further analyzed to identify products with repeated complaints and determine the most frequently mentioned customer concerns.

## 4. Ebook Customer Feedback

Ebook recorded an average rating of 4.11 with a negative sentiment percentage of 9.21%.

The lower negative-review percentage indicates comparatively fewer negative reviews in the analyzed dataset.

### Business interpretation

Further analysis can focus on the specific products and issues mentioned in the negative reviews rather than treating the entire category as a single group.

## 5. Books Customer Feedback

Books recorded an average rating of 4.34 with a negative sentiment percentage of 6.43%.

The negative-review analysis can be used to identify individual books that receive repeated complaints.

### Business interpretation

Product-level analysis can help distinguish isolated complaints from recurring issues affecting particular products.

## 6. Grocery Customer Feedback

Grocery recorded an average rating of 4.17.

The Grocery dataset also required sentiment-value normalization during the SQL analysis process. Therefore, sentiment-based business interpretation should use the corrected and validated sentiment results.

### Business interpretation

The category can be further investigated using product-level ratings, negative reviews, helpful votes, and issue keywords.

## 7. Customer Pain Points

The negative-review keyword analysis focused on:

- Product quality
- Product condition
- Authenticity
- Price
- Delivery

These themes provide potential areas for business teams to investigate.

## 8. Product-Level Investigation

Products with higher numbers of negative reviews were identified using SQL.

The next level of analysis should examine:

1. Number of negative reviews
2. Average rating
3. Helpful votes
4. Common complaint keywords
5. Category
6. Product title

This can help identify recurring customer issues at the product level.

## 9. Recommended Business Actions

Based on the analysis, possible actions include:

- Investigate products with repeated negative reviews.
- Review recurring quality and product-condition complaints.
- Examine authenticity-related complaints.
- Investigate price-related negative feedback.
- Review delivery-related complaints.
- Use highly helpful negative reviews to prioritize customer issues.
- Monitor category-level sentiment and rating changes over time.
